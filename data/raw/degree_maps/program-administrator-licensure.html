Your advisor and the licensure office will review your prior coursework to determine whether additional courses are needed to meet licensure requirements.

Internship and Portfolio

Candidates complete an administrative internship and project as part of the program. Before enrolling in the internship, they must meet the applicable coursework, practicum, application, and program requirements. Candidates must also prepare and satisfactorily defend a portfolio aligned with the appropriate administrative licensure standards before applying for licensure.
