Or other courses not on the above list in which:
a significant amount of material would fall under Africana Studies, or students engage in a major project related to Africana Studies.
Examples: Students studying or participating in plays by African American playwrights in theatre classes, students studying jazz, hip hop, or other music or dance with African roots in music or dance classes, students studying Afro-Germans in a Modern Germany class, or students studying African American farmers in an AGED course, etc.
The Department of History, Political Science, and Geography chair will decide if a course or project will qualify.
