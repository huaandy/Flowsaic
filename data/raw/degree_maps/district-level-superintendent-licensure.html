EDAS 6303 – Superintendent Internship and Graduate Project

Internship and Portfolio

The program includes a superintendent internship and graduate project. Candidates must meet the applicable coursework, practicum, and internship application requirements before enrolling. They must prepare and satisfactorily defend an administrative portfolio before applying for licensure.
